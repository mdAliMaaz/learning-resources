# Part 3: Study Philosophy

**Learn from first principles. Build things from scratch. Understand the underlying mathematics and computer science.**

Prioritize:

1. Mathematical foundations
2. Computer architecture and operating systems
3. Algorithms and data structures
4. Networking and distributed systems
5. Databases and storage engines
6. Probability and information theory
7. Machine learning and AI engineering

The objective is not simply to complete courses, but to develop a deep understanding of how things work internally.
